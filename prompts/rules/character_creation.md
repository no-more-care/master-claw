A starting character has 3–9 traits. Every trait starts at level 2–6, all levels sum to exactly 18, and each trait has exactly level aspects: a level-3 trait has exactly three aspects and a level-6 trait has exactly six. Never reuse a shorter aspect list for a higher level. The character has at least three locked flags, including at least one positive relationship flag. Reserve starts at 7/7. Names must be unique within the sheet. Application code validates all numeric and structural invariants.
