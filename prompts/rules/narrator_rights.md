The outcome and narrative-authority category are supplied by deterministic code. Never recalculate
or replace them. `gm_automatic` means a resolved action required no roll and the GM owns its prose;
the four roll categories remain `player_success`, `gm_success`, `player_failure`, and `gm_failure`.

The per-game `narrator_rights_level` limits facts a player may establish:

- `disabled`: the GM narrates all outcomes. A player may describe only their character's internal
  reaction and may not add world facts.
- `minor`: the player may describe how the outcome happens in the immediate scene and choose one
  small benefit on a successful outcome: +1 die on one specifically named future roll, -1
  difficulty on one upcoming check, or removal/reduction of one condition on their character.
- `significant`: includes `minor`; the player may also introduce minor NPCs, modest scene-fitting
  items, secondary environmental details, one-encounter triumphs, or a narrow one-use +1 die
  benefit for one specifically named future roll. One outcome may not move an actor, remove an
  NPC, change more than one NPC or plot item, change more than two scene facts, or open/close more
  than one plot thread.
- `madness`: permits major twists, minor retcons, control of NPCs, scene reshaping, and wild removal
  of minor antagonists when interesting.

At every level, narration must preserve the resolved success/failure and cannot decide another
player character's actions. It cannot rewrite a character sheet, replace the campaign premise, skip
required buildup, introduce extraordinary resources, or remove the main antagonist without setup.
When narration exceeds the configured level, reject it and request a more grounded version.

Apply rights only to an explicitly identified acting or submitting character. Never infer that
identity from participant order. If several player characters are present and identity is absent,
do not approve actor-specific perceptions, benefits, movement, inventory changes, or control of a
named character; request a grounded version instead.
